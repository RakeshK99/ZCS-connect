ZCS CONNECT

Privacy policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Beta. This policy covers the ZCS Connect beta on TestFlight, which runs a fictional demo school. Zeeland Christian School will publish the policy for the full release.

ZCS Connect lets Zeeland Christian School ("ZCS", "the school") share announcements, the school calendar and teacher messages with families. This page explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address, which come from the school's roster or from the account you sign in with (Apple, Google or an emailed code).
  • Your family's connection to the school: which children you're a guardian of, and their grades and classes. This comes from the school's roster; you can't add or change it in the app.
  • What you send: messages to teachers, read confirmations and photos staff attach to posts.
  • Notification details: a push notification token for your device and your notification settings (such as quiet hours).
  • Basic records of which posts were delivered and opened, so the school knows whether an important notice reached every family.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no selling or renting your information.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking across other apps and websites.
  • No student accounts. Children don't use the app.

How it's used

Only to run the app: to show you posts and events for your children, deliver notifications and email, and let you message your children's teachers. Staff see only the families and classes their role covers.

Message oversight

To keep everyone safe, school administrators may review messages between parents and teachers. Every review is recorded. The Messages screen in the app reminds you of this.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase stores the app's data (United States).
  • Expo and Apple or Google deliver push notifications.
  • Resend delivers email.

They process data only to provide these services.

How long we keep it

For as long as your family is connected to the school. When you delete your account, your sign-in and devices are removed; messages already sent stay with the school's records. The demo school used in the beta is deleted when the beta ends.

Your choices

  • Turn notifications off, or set quiet hours, in Profile. Emergency alerts always come through.
  • Delete your account any time in Profile → Delete account.
  • Ask the school office to correct roster details.
