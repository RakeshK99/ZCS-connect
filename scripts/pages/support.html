Help and support

For families and staff of Zeeland Christian School

Trying the beta

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store, then open the beta invite link on your iPhone.
  2. Open ZCS Connect and tap Get started.
  3. In the Playground: demo school box, pick a person to try the app as, and enter the demo password you were given.

The beta uses a fictional school, shared by everyone testing. Please don't post real names, photos of children or personal details. Push notifications and email aren't switched on yet.

Who to try

  • Sarah VanDyke: a parent with two children.
  • Jennifer Smith: a teacher who is also a parent. Use the switcher at the top to change between them.
  • David Kuiper (principal) and Linda Brouwer (office): post to grades or the whole school.
  • New parent: what a family not yet on the roster sees.

Sending feedback

Take a screenshot in the app, tap Share Beta Feedback, add a note and send. You can also send feedback from the TestFlight app, or email rakesh@syzygy.services.

Questions about your family's details

Children, classes and contact details come from the school office's roster. If something is wrong, contact the ZCS office.
